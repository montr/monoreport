# DOCX Template Compatibility

> **Status:** Approved Feature 011 contract, incorporating Product Owner decisions D1–D6 approved on 2026-10-19. The current source implements this policy and automated follow-up verification is complete; **Feature 011 was accepted by the Product Owner on 2026-10-19**. This policy does not retroactively change accepted Feature 010 requirements and evidence.

MonoReport generates printable documents from DOCX templates and data. It is not a general-purpose Word or OOXML editor. The goal is to support practical report and print-form templates while preserving unrelated Word content wherever safe.

## Compatibility rules

Each Word feature is classified by its behavior in context. A feature may be preserved in one situation and rejected in another.

| Word feature | MonoReport behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Plain text and character/paragraph formatting | **Supported.** Replace MonoReport expressions while preserving the surrounding Word formatting within the documented template syntax and rendering constraints. |
| Tables | **Supported.** Render supported expressions, loops, and conditions in table content; repeat supported table rows and other documented structural units. `For`/`If` retain existing row-level vertical-merge restrictions. Raw supports multiple paragraphs in one cell, complete merged tables, and unchanged merged body rows; reject only Raw delimiter deletion that compromises merges or section boundaries. Raw, `For`, `If`, and their combinations may leave no authored cell content. Generation adds one empty paragraph only when an affected cell would otherwise be empty or end in a nested table. No merge repair, section-property relocation, or general DOCX repair is performed. |
| Images | **Supported.** Preserve images and relationships in ordinary and repeated content, including Raw inside loops. Keep physical-copy drawing IDs unique across the package, reserving IDs in unchanged comments, glossary parts, and other already inspected XML. |
| Lists and numbering | **Preserved unchanged.** Retain Word list formatting and numbering definitions. Automatic numbering restarts or recalculation are not guaranteed. |
| Word comments | **Preserved unchanged.** Unaffected comments, valid comments within or spanning Raw, and ranges fully enclosing `For`/`If` when endpoints and required attachments remain intact outside the operation and the resulting range stays valid. Enclosing-range support is conditional on simple preservation, with no cloning or reconstruction.<br><br>**Removed safely.** A complete ranged or reference-only point attachment wholly on one removed Raw delimiter container disappears with it; handle definitions/references without leaving dangling structures and preserve shared definitions needed by surviving attachments.<br><br>**Rejected.** Local attachments within `For`/`If` bodies or markers, and split/crossing arrangements that cannot survive unchanged, including deletion that leaves an invalid range. Raw inside an enclosing operation does not bypass these restrictions. No comment or definition cloning, identity remapping, or relocation. |
| Bookmarks | **Preserved unchanged.** Unaffected bookmarks, valid bookmarks within or spanning Raw, and ranges fully enclosing `For`/`If` whose endpoints remain intact outside the operation and whose resulting range stays valid. Enclosing-range support is conditional on simple preservation.<br><br>**Removed safely.** A complete bookmark wholly on one removed Raw delimiter container disappears with it; reject retained internal links to that removed target.<br><br>**Rejected.** Local bookmarks within `For`/`If` bodies or markers, or split/crossing arrangements that cannot survive unchanged. No dynamic creation, renaming, relocation, or reconstruction. |
| Permission ranges | **Preserve existing Feature 010 behavior.** Match complete endpoints within independent story roots; allow ranges local to one loop body/conditional branch, fully enclosing ranges, and self-contained deleted-marker ranges. Allocate matching local IDs for physical copies; reject affected split, crossing, duplicate, missing, or misordered endpoints. Raw alone does not clone ranges and must not split surviving endpoints. This retained support is distinct from rejected bookmark/comment cloning. |
| Internal hyperlinks | **Preserved unchanged.** Explicit internal links outside `For`/`If` footprints to valid unchanged bookmarks, including preserved enclosing bookmarks and valid Raw body targets. Preserve unrelated pre-existing unresolved anchors outside affected operations.<br><br>**Rejected.** A link inside a `For`/`If` footprint even if its target is stable, or a retained link whose target is removed by Raw delimiter/annotation deletion, repeated, ambiguous, or otherwise invalidated. Checks do not depend on actual branch selection or iteration count. No retargeting or dynamic remapping. Field-based links remain opaque under the fields rule. |
| External hyperlinks | **Preserved unchanged.** External hyperlink relationships, addresses, and fragments/anchors, including relationship-based links with `w:anchor`. They are not treated as internal bookmark references. |
| Automatic table of contents | **Preserved unchanged.** Word fields and their cached display content are not recalculated by MonoReport. Users update the table of contents in Word when necessary.<br><br>**Rejected.** Any associated bookmark or link structure that violates the structural-operation restrictions above. |
| Word cross-references and other fields | **Preserved unchanged.** Field instructions and cached results are not evaluated or refreshed by MonoReport. Fields are opaque to template expression substitution. This includes `REF`, `PAGEREF`, and field-based hyperlinks. |
| Content controls (`w:sdt`) | **Preserved unchanged.** Content-control subtrees, including bindings and contained text, are opaque: MonoReport does not interpret template expressions inside them. Content controls must not cause expressions outside them to be interpreted across their boundaries. |
| Tracked changes and revision markup | **Rejected within the current inspection footprint.** Recognized unresolved revisions in the main document, headers, footers, footnotes, and endnotes produce actionable diagnostics, including inside Raw, inactive branches, and content controls. Retain the current recognized content/cell, move/custom-XML range, deleted-payload, property-change, and Office 2010 conflict families and drawing/picture exclusions. No package-wide or auxiliary-part revision scan is introduced. Merely enabling Track Changes without unresolved revisions is allowed. |

## Raw delimiter and preservation rules

1. Raw uses exact, case-sensitive `{Raw}` and `{/Raw}` markers in dedicated sibling paragraphs under one exact parent or supported dedicated sibling rows of one table. A row marker occupies one paragraph in one cell; other visible row content is empty. Existing whitespace and split-run rules apply. Paragraph delimiters cannot pair across different cells; row delimiters must belong to the same table. Mixed paragraph/row and cross-part/root layouts are invalid. A delimiter's text cannot be assembled across opaque content boundaries; a valid Raw body may contain complete opaque constructs.
2. Inside Raw, all template-language constructs, including malformed expressions, controls, formatters, and brace escapes, are literal. They produce no language validation, evaluation, dependency extraction, actionable occurrences, or content-derived warnings. Only the first exact, unescaped, structurally valid eligible closing marker ends Raw. Inline, wrong-parent, mixed-content, escaped, or opaque lookalikes remain literal; another opening marker is literal because Raw does not nest. Missing a valid close is an error. Match by eligible container placement, then report integrity/compatibility failures rather than skipping that closer to consume a larger region.
3. Raw may be nested inside `For`/`If` combinations but must lie within one enclosing body/branch. Literal controls do not alter the outer stack. Actual outer branch/end markers occur outside Raw. Raw adds no data context; ordinary processing resumes after it.
4. Raw removes its delimiters and the safe delimiter-local annotations described above, while preserving body XML and formatting. Enclosing operations may clone or exclude that body; their restrictions still apply even for inactive branches or zero iterations. Opacity does not disable DOCX integrity, revision, or range/reference validation.
5. Support paragraph-delimited Raw containing complete tables, including merged cells, and Raw spanning multiple paragraphs within one cell. Preserve row-delimited Raw bodies, including merges that survive delimiter deletion. Reject deletion that would break a merge chain or remove a section boundary. Empty results from Raw, `For`, `If`, and their combinations are valid in table cells; generation adds a bare empty paragraph when an affected cell would otherwise contain no blocks or would end with a nested table. This narrow Word-compatibility correction is the explicit exception to the no-synthesized-content rule and does not authorize other repair or relocation.
6. For delimiter-local comment removal, retain definitions still needed by surviving attachments/references, and remove a deleted annotation's definition only when doing so leaves no dangling structures. Preserve unrelated comments, media, and relationships; do not perform general package cleanup. Do not add annotation relocation, identity cloning, or link-remapping mechanisms. If safe deletion requires significant additional machinery, present the tradeoff for Product Owner review before proceeding.

## Validation and diagnostics

1. **Validate before generating.** Analyze and Generate must apply the same compatibility rules. Unsupported structures must produce actionable diagnostics; Generate must not silently produce a corrupt DOCX.
2. **Explain the remedy.** Diagnostics should identify the affected Word feature and location, and tell the author what to change in Word (for example, remove a comment from a loop, move a bookmark outside a loop, or accept tracked changes).
3. **Preserve rather than interpret.** Features designated *Preserved unchanged* are not evaluated, updated, or automatically repaired by MonoReport.
4. **Keep structural boundaries explicit.** A `For`/`If` footprint includes opening, optional Else, closing containers, both bodies, and descendants. Complete ranges enclosing the operation may survive unchanged when endpoints and required attachments are outside that footprint; split or local affected attachments remain restricted. Raw performs delimiter deletion and has its own safe-preservation rules, not a blanket prohibition on constructs in its body.
5. **No implicit fallback.** Do not silently choose the first or last generated bookmark, share a repeated comment identity, or rewrite an external hyperlink fragment to make an unsupported template appear to work.
6. **Respect complexity conditions.** Preserve fully enclosing comments/bookmarks without cloning, renaming, relocation, or reconstruction. If this needs substantial extra logic, recommend actionable rejection and obtain Product Owner confirmation before changing the contract. Significant machinery for Raw annotation deletion likewise requires a Product Owner tradeoff decision; do not silently expand scope or reject all delimiter-attached annotations.
7. **Keep checks proportionate.** Apply shared data-independent preflight, retain the existing revision inspection footprint, and use source identities/locations for boundary and reference checks. Do not reject a template solely because supported block rendering can empty a cell. The targeted rendering correction above does not introduce a universal OOXML repair framework or require exhaustive capability coverage. Preserve unrelated opaque content where safe.

3. Feature 011 aligns Core, Analyzer, CLI/API/Playground behavior, maintained executable assets, and current user documentation with this policy. Obsolete clone/remap mechanisms are removed after replacement rejection and preservation checks; permission/drawing identity safety and narrowly required Raw cleanup remain.
4. Focused acceptance checks and existing meaningful regressions verify safe preservation/deletion, shared rejection and no partial outputs, applicable Open XML validity, and semantic references/content. Manual Word verification limits must be disclosed. Future changes to supported Word features require corresponding approved specification, policy, and test updates.
